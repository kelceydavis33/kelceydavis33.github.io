## [STEMArts](https://stemarts.com/sciart-productions/sciart-live-coding/) Scientist
Summer 2025 – present

![Presenting an audio file and the CAPERS spectrum it was generated from](livecoding.jpeg)

- Sonified JWST NIRSpec data from the CAPERS program into audio of emission
  lines, and computed RGB values for rest-frame line colors, so students could
  build audiovisual performances from real spectra.
- Worked alongside live-coding composer Alexandra Cárdenas, visual artist
  MAGENTA, and a STEMArts team led by Agnes Chavez.
- Lectured on spectroscopy, redshift and emission line galaxies so students
  understood the data they were performing, and gave a public talk at the Taos
  film festival where they premiered their pieces.
